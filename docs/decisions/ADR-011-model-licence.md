# ADR-011: Model licence and what v2.0.0 releases

Status: Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-05: release scope, names and licence. Applied in spec 2.0.0-spec.9.
Date: 2026-11-05
Amends: ADR-004 (model weights), once accepted

## Context

ADR-004 keeps the weights' licence pending until every training dataset's terms are audited. The register is [`docs/data_licenses.md`](../data_licenses.md), and the licence files were read on 2026-11-05. This is a reading of their text, not legal advice.

V2 lost the registered text-to-data comparison (EXP-M4-T2D). By the rule registered before the run, its case rests on reproducibility, so the card leads with that result.

## Decision

Decided by the owner:
- **Release both `LiteGPT-V2-Base` and `LiteGPT-Llama-V2-Instruct`.** The owner first chose no "Llama" in the name, then chose this form over `Llama-LiteGPT-V2-Instruct`, knowing the licence text asks for the word at the beginning.
- **Weights: Apache-2.0**, each with its notice file: [Base](../release/NOTICE.txt), [Instruct](../release/NOTICE_instruct.txt). **Code: MIT**, unchanged.
- **Display:** Instruct's documentation shows "Built with Llama" and "Built with Qwen" and keeps the Llama notice. Base shows "Built with Qwen".
- **Not released:** the text-to-data fine-tunes, whose results are published.
- **Where:** code on `kmkrofficial/LiteGPT`, weights on `kmkrworks` on Hugging Face.

## The Instruct name

Llama 3.1 section 1(b)(i) reads: "If you use the Llama Materials or any outputs or results of the Llama Materials to create, train, fine tune, or otherwise improve an AI model, which is distributed or made available, you shall also include 'Llama' at the beginning of any such AI model name."

V2-Instruct was fine-tuned on smol-magpie-ultra, generated by Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct, so as written the clause reaches it. `LiteGPT-Llama-V2-Instruct` includes the word but doesn't start with it. `Llama-LiteGPT-V2-Instruct` is the form that matches the text exactly. The owner chose the first, and that gap is a known risk.

What bears on it:
- The dataset card labels smol-magpie-ultra Apache-2.0 and names Llama as its generator.
- It is unsettled how far a licence on a generator's outputs binds someone who only trained on a public dataset of them.
- SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, fine-tuned on `smol-smoltalk`, ships under Apache-2.0 with no Llama in its name. This is a precedent, not legal cover.

The risk stays with the publisher: a licence claim or a takedown request. A name can be changed later without retraining.

## Alternatives rejected

- **No Llama in the name:** contrary to the text as written. The owner dropped this.
- **Holding Instruct back:** removes the risk. The owner wants both released.
- **Retraining Instruct without smol-magpie-ultra:** only about 11M tokens of chat data would remain, too few.
- **A custom or non-commercial licence:** nothing in the audited terms requires one.

## Consequences

Residual risks are accepted and stated, not solved:
- Common Crawl's terms name AI training in an indemnity clause and carry binding arbitration.
- Code licence labels are repository-level, so individual files may differ, and their attributions are not reproduced.
- Instruct refuses nothing, and its card says so first.

Spec 2.0.0-spec.9 records the licence. Earlier run records keep the spec hash they ran under.
