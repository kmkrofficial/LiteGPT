# ADR-011: Model licence and what v2.0.0 releases

Status: Proposed. The owner has not approved it, and ADR-004's release gate stays closed until they do.
Date: 2026-11-05
Amends: ADR-004 (model weights), once accepted

## Context

ADR-004 keeps the weights' licence pending until every training dataset's terms are audited. The register is [`docs/data_licenses.md`](../data_licenses.md). The licence files were read on 2026-11-05. This is a reading of their text, not legal advice, and it is unclear how far a licence on a generator's outputs binds someone who only trained on a public dataset of them.

V2 lost the registered text-to-data comparison (EXP-M4-T2D). By the rule registered before the run, its case rests on reproducibility only.

## Decision (proposed)

- **Release LiteGPT-V2-Base only**, under that name, as a research model.
- **Weights: Apache-2.0, with a [NOTICE](../release/NOTICE.txt)**: "Built with Qwen", the ODC-By notices, and the Common Crawl and code-label statements. **Code: MIT**, unchanged.
- **V2-Instruct is not distributed.** It trained on smol-magpie-ultra, generated by Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct. Llama 3.1 section 1(b)(i) asks that a distributed model trained on such outputs start its name with "Llama". The owner does not want that name, so it stays private. Its results are published.
- **Text-to-data fine-tunes are not distributed.** Their results are published.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Publishing Instruct as `LiteGPT-V2-Instruct`:** contrary to the licence text as written, and a legal risk the project has no reason to take for a model weaker than free alternatives.
- **Retraining Instruct without smol-magpie-ultra:** only about 11M tokens of chat data would remain, too few.
- **A custom or non-commercial licence:** nothing in the audited terms requires one.

## Consequences

Residual risks are accepted and stated, not solved:
- Common Crawl's terms name AI training in an indemnity clause and carry binding arbitration.
- Code licence labels are repository-level, so individual files may differ, and their attributions are not reproduced.
- The Qwen clause applies if outputs bind a downstream trainer. The notice is included either way.

Spec field `licensing.model` changes with a spec bump when this is accepted.
